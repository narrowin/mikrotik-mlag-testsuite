### MLAG secondary reboot · Medium lab, MSTP, access switch as root · MSTP

Resets all three bridges over management SSH with protocol-mode=mstp, priority 0x1000 on CHR 1 and CHR 2 and 0x0000 on the access switch. It requires a loss-free baseline with the access switch as root (0x0000.02:00:00:21:00:13). It then reboots CHR 2 (MLAG secondary) with `/system reboot`, waits for the guest uptime to reset and keeps probing for 16 s plus a 3 s recovery window. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case medium-mstp-access-root-reboot-secondary`.

| Phase | Expected | Limit | Run 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Secondary down | CHR 1 keeps the shared system ID, so the access switch keeps its LACP partner and stays RSTP root. | ≤ 5 s | ⚠️ INVALID · no loss |
| Secondary back | CHR 2 keeps its bond members out of the bond until the MLAG system ID syncs (mlag-init-delay), then adds them under the unchanged shared system ID. | ≤ 5 s | ⚠️ INVALID · no loss · [failed check](01-medium-mstp-access-root-reboot-secondary/result.json#L188) |

- **Secondary down:** CHR 1 keeps the shared system ID, so the access switch keeps its LACP partner and stays RSTP root. The MLAG pair keeps access-uplink as its forwarding root port. The virtual links keep carrier, so the access switch and traffic host 2 drop their member to CHR 2 at the LACP short timeout (3 × 1 s), with brief loss on traffic hashed to it. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
- **Secondary back:** CHR 2 keeps its bond members out of the bond until the MLAG system ID syncs (mlag-init-delay), then adds them under the unchanged shared system ID. The access switch stays root, and CHR 2's non-edge access-uplink must reach forwarding without a further outage. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
