### MLAG bond member loss and return · MSTP

Takes client 1's bond member to CHR 1 (ether3) down at both ends, holds it down for 8 s, then brings both ends back up. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case bond-member-loss`.

| Phase | Expected | Limit | Run 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Member down | Carrier drops at both ends, so CHR 1's MLAG bond client1 (mlag-id=11) stops using ether3 and client 1 sends only on its member to CHR 2. | ≤ 3 s | ✅ PASS · up to 0.21 s (1 probe) |
| Member back | Carrier returns at both ends and LACP renegotiates ether3 with client 1 at the 1-second LACPDU rate. | ≤ 3 s | ✅ PASS · 2.43 to 2.64 s (24 probes) |

- **Member down:** Carrier drops at both ends, so CHR 1's MLAG bond client1 (mlag-id=11) stops using ether3 and client 1 sends only on its member to CHR 2. CHR 1 forwards traffic for client 1 over the peer link (mlag-peer-port ether2) to CHR 2, which delivers it on its member of the same MLAG bond, with brief loss. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
- **Member back:** Carrier returns at both ends and LACP renegotiates ether3 with client 1 at the 1-second LACPDU rate. Each end carries traffic on the member only once its LACP flags show synchronization, collecting and distributing (S, C, D), so the return causes at most brief loss. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/cli-reference/interface/bonding/monitor-slaves/)

### LACP partner timeout and return · MSTP

Blocks client 1's LACPDUs towards CHR 1 on ether3 for 8 s while carrier stays up, then lets them through again. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case lacp-timeout`.

| Phase | Expected | Limit | Run 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LACPDUs blocked | Carrier stays up, but CHR 1 receives no LACPDUs on ether3. | ≤ 4 s | ✅ PASS · 3.23 to 3.44 s (32 probes) |
| LACPDUs resumed | Client 1's LACPDUs reach CHR 1 again, so ether3 has current partner information and clears the expired (E) and defaulted (F) flags. | ≤ 3 s | ✅ PASS · 0.21 to 0.42 s (3 probes) |

- **LACPDUs blocked:** Carrier stays up, but CHR 1 receives no LACPDUs on ether3. With lacp-rate=1sec, CHR 1's partner information expires after the 3 s short timeout: ether3 shows expired (E) and stops collecting and distributing, and traffic for client 1 moves to its member on CHR 2, crossing the peer port where needed. [Source](https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/802.1AX/6768/)
- **LACPDUs resumed:** Client 1's LACPDUs reach CHR 1 again, so ether3 has current partner information and clears the expired (E) and defaulted (F) flags. The member returns to synchronization, collecting and distributing (S, C, D) in the MLAG bond, with brief loss. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/cli-reference/interface/bonding/monitor-slaves/)

### MAC move between MLAG peers and back · MSTP

Switches the MAC-move host's active-backup bond from its link to CHR 1 to its link to CHR 2, keeping the same MAC and IP address, then switches it back after 4 s. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case mac-move`.

| Phase | Expected | Limit | Run 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move to CHR 2 | The host's gratuitous ARP enters CHR 2 on ether5, a regular bridge port without mlag-id. | no loss | ✅ PASS · no loss |
| Move back to CHR 1 | The host's gratuitous ARP enters CHR 1 on ether5. | no loss | ✅ PASS · no loss |

- **Move to CHR 2:** The host's gratuitous ARP enters CHR 2 on ether5, a regular bridge port without mlag-id. CHR 2 moves the host MAC to ether5 in its bridge host table and floods the broadcast to its local ports and over the peer port; CHR 1 floods it only to its regular port ether5 and moves the MAC to the peer port ether2. Lab target: no probe to or from the host is lost, and once the gratuitous ARP reaches CHR 2, no frame addressed to the host MAC leaves CHR 1 on ether5.
- **Move back to CHR 1:** The host's gratuitous ARP enters CHR 1 on ether5. CHR 1 moves the host MAC back to ether5 and floods the broadcast to its local ports and over the peer port; CHR 2 floods it only to its regular port ether5 and moves the MAC to the peer port ether2. Lab target: no probe to or from the host is lost, and from the moment the gratuitous ARP reaches CHR 1 to the end of the recovery window, no frame addressed to the host MAC leaves CHR 2 on ether5.

### Peer link loss and return · MSTP

Cuts the peer link (ether2) at both MLAG peers, holds it down for 15 s, restores it and watches 65 s of return. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case peer-link-loss`.

| Peer link down | Both MLAG peers lose ICCP and leave mlag-state connected. | ≤ 5 s | ✅ PASS · no loss |

- **Peer link down:** Both MLAG peers lose ICCP and leave mlag-state connected. With mlag-lacp-fallback=yes the MLAG secondary stops using the shared system ID, so the clients renegotiate LACP and keep their member to the primary, with brief loss. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
