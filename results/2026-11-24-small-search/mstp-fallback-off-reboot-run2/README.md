### MLAG secondary reboot · MSTP

Resets the bridge on CHR 1 and CHR 2 over management SSH with the bridge mode of the run, picks the MLAG peer that reports mlag-state connected as secondary, and after a loss-free baseline reboots it with `/system reboot` (1 s delay). The runner waits for the guest uptime to reset, then keeps probing for 16 s plus a 3 s recovery window. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case secondary-reboot`.

| Phase | Expected | Limit | Run 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Secondary down | The MLAG primary keeps the shared system ID, so both traffic hosts keep their LACP partner and forward over their bond member to the primary, which delivers locally without the peer port. | ≤ 5 s | ⚠️ INVALID · no loss |
| Secondary back | The rejoining secondary keeps its bond members out of the 802.3ad bond until the MLAG system ID syncs with the primary (mlag-init-delay, default 1 min), then adds them under the unchanged shared system ID. | ≤ 5 s | ⚠️ INVALID · no loss · [failed check](01-secondary-reboot/result.json#L178) |

- **Secondary down:** The MLAG primary keeps the shared system ID, so both traffic hosts keep their LACP partner and forward over their bond member to the primary, which delivers locally without the peer port. The virtual links keep carrier, so each host drops its member to the secondary only at the LACP short timeout (3 × 1 s). Only traffic hashed to that member sees brief loss. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
- **Secondary back:** The rejoining secondary keeps its bond members out of the 802.3ad bond until the MLAG system ID syncs with the primary (mlag-init-delay, default 1 min), then adds them under the unchanged shared system ID. The MLAG bonds client1 and client2 are edge ports and forward at once, so loss is brief or none. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
