### MLAG secondary power loss · Medium lab, all bridges STP off · STP off

Resets all three bridges over management SSH with protocol-mode=none. It requires a loss-free probe baseline, zero BPDUs and one copy of each broadcast. It then emulates a power loss of CHR 2 (MLAG secondary, mlag-priority=20): in one step it takes the container veths behind CHR 2's ether2 to ether4 down, sets the facing NICs of CHR 1 and the access switch link-down over QMP and pauses CHR 2's guest with QMP stop, then confirms carrier loss at every neighbour. After 20 s it powers CHR 2 on with QMP system_reset and cont, brings the links back up, waits for the guest uptime to reset and keeps probing for 16 s plus a 3 s recovery window. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case medium-stp-off-power-loss-secondary`.

| Phase | Expected | Limit | Run 1 | Run 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Secondary off | Every link to CHR 2 loses carrier at once, as on a real power loss. | ≤ 5 s | ✅ PASS · up to 0.15 s | ✅ PASS · up to 0.13 s |
| Secondary back | Carrier returns while CHR 2 boots. | ≤ 5 s | ✅ PASS · no loss | ✅ PASS · up to 0.21 s |

- **Secondary off:** Every link to CHR 2 loses carrier at once, as on a real power loss. CHR 1 (MLAG primary) sees its peer port ether2 go down and keeps the shared system ID, so the access switch's uplink bond and traffic host 2 keep their LACP partner. They drop their member to CHR 2 on carrier loss, without waiting for the LACP short timeout, and forward over their members to CHR 1. With protocol-mode=none no port changes state and no BPDUs appear. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
- **Secondary back:** Carrier returns while CHR 2 boots. CHR 2 keeps its bond members out of the 802.3ad bond until the MLAG system ID syncs with CHR 1 (mlag-init-delay), then adds them under the unchanged shared system ID. The access switch and traffic host 2 re-add the member to the same aggregator, so loss is brief or none. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
