## Cases

### Inter-pair bond member flap · two MLAG pairs, spanning tree off · STP off

Resets the data bridge on all four CHRs over management SSH with protocol-mode=none. Pair A (CHR 1 MLAG primary, CHR 2 MLAG secondary) and pair B (CHR 3 MLAG primary, CHR 4 MLAG secondary) are joined by one distributed LACP bond, mlag-id 11 on pair A and 21 on pair B. Host 1 bonds to pair B and host 2 to pair A, so both VLAN 110 streams cross both pairs. It requires a loss-free baseline. It then takes the inter-pair bond member between CHR 1 ether3 and CHR 3 ether3 down at both ends (both container veths and both QEMU NICs), holds it down for 8 s, brings both ends back up and watches 8 s of return plus a 3 s recovery window. The bridge host table size and the topology-change counter of every CHR are sampled throughout. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case two-pair-stp-off-interpair-member-flap`.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Inter-pair member back | Carrier returns at both ends and LACP adds ether3 back to the inter-pair bond on CHR 1 and CHR 3 once it shows synchronization, collecting and distributing (S, C, D). | ≤ 3 s | ✅ PASS · 0.10 to 0.31 s (2 probes) |

- **Inter-pair member down:** CHR 1 and CHR 3 each lose their only member of the inter-pair MLAG bond (mlag-id 11 on pair A, 21 on pair B). The bond keeps forwarding over its other member between CHR 2 and CHR 4, and traffic that reaches CHR 1 or CHR 3 crosses its pair's peer port. Streams 101 and 201 lose at most 3 s. No bridge sends or forwards BPDUs. The field report saw unrelated MACs move to the peer port and the host table shrink after this flap; the host table size and topology-change counter of every CHR are recorded for comparison. [Source](https://forum.mikrotik.com/t/7-25-1-stable-is-released/274226/22)
- **Inter-pair member back:** Carrier returns at both ends and LACP adds ether3 back to the inter-pair bond on CHR 1 and CHR 3 once it shows synchronization, collecting and distributing (S, C, D). Streams 101 and 201 lose at most 3 s. No bridge sends or forwards BPDUs. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/cli-reference/interface/bonding/monitor-slaves/)
- **MikroTik fixes this case checks:** Packets looped back to a dual-connected bond ([7.25.2](https://cdn.mikrotik.com/routeros/7.25.2/CHANGELOG)); BPDUs leaking to the wrong dual-connected bond (introduced in 7.24) ([7.24.6](https://cdn.mikrotik.com/routeros/7.24.6/CHANGELOG)); LACP system ID not synchronised when bonds are created before MLAG ([7.25.2](https://cdn.mikrotik.com/routeros/7.25.2/CHANGELOG))
