- **Secondary down:** The MLAG primary keeps the shared system ID, so both traffic hosts keep their LACP partner and forward over their bond member to the primary, which delivers locally without the peer port. ### MLAG secondary power loss · STP off

Resets the bridge on CHR 1 and CHR 2 over management SSH with the bridge mode of the run, picks the MLAG peer that reports mlag-state connected as secondary, and after a loss-free baseline emulates its power loss. In one step it takes the container veths behind the secondary's ether2 to ether5 down, sets the other peer's peer-link NIC link-down over QMP and pauses the secondary's guest with QMP stop, then confirms carrier loss at every neighbour. After 20 s it powers the secondary on with QMP system_reset and cont, brings the links back up, waits for the guest uptime to reset and keeps probing for 16 s plus a 3 s recovery window. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case secondary-power-loss`.

| Phase | Expected | Limit | Run 1 | Run 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Secondary off | Every link to the secondary loses carrier at once, as on a real power loss. | ≤ 5 s | ❌ FAIL · no loss · [failed check](01-secondary-power-loss/result.json#L431) | ❌ FAIL · no loss · [failed check](02-secondary-power-loss/result.json#L431) |
| Secondary back | Carrier returns while the secondary boots. | ≤ 5 s | ✅ PASS · no loss | ✅ PASS · no loss |

- **Secondary off:** Every link to the secondary loses carrier at once, as on a real power loss. The MLAG primary sees its peer port ether2 go down and keeps the shared system ID, so both traffic hosts keep their LACP partner. Each host drops its member to the secondary on carrier loss, without waiting for the LACP short timeout, and forwards over its member to the primary. Only traffic hashed to the lost member sees brief loss. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
- **Secondary back:** Carrier returns while the secondary boots. Its bond members stay out of the 802.3ad bond until the MLAG system ID syncs with the primary (mlag-init-delay, default 1 min), then join under the unchanged shared system ID. The MLAG bonds client1 and client2 are edge ports and forward at once, so loss is brief or none. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
