### Baseline · two MLAG pairs, spanning tree off · STP off

Resets the data bridge on all four CHRs over management SSH with protocol-mode=none. Pair A (CHR 1 MLAG primary, CHR 2 MLAG secondary) and pair B (CHR 3 MLAG primary, CHR 4 MLAG secondary) are joined by one distributed LACP bond, mlag-id 11 on pair A and 21 on pair B. Host 1 bonds to pair B and host 2 to pair A, so both VLAN 110 streams cross both pairs. Then runs numbered 100 ms pings for 4 s in both VLAN 110 and both bypass directions and reads back bridge, MLAG and LACP state on all four CHRs. No fault is applied. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case two-pair-stp-off-baseline`.

### Pair A primary reboot · two MLAG pairs, spanning tree off · STP off

Resets the data bridge on all four CHRs over management SSH with protocol-mode=none. Pair A (CHR 1 MLAG primary, CHR 2 MLAG secondary) and pair B (CHR 3 MLAG primary, CHR 4 MLAG secondary) are joined by one distributed LACP bond, mlag-id 11 on pair A and 21 on pair B. Host 1 bonds to pair B and host 2 to pair A, so both VLAN 110 streams cross both pairs. It requires a loss-free baseline and zero BPDUs. It then reboots CHR 1 (pair A's MLAG primary) with `/system reboot`, waits for the guest uptime to reset and keeps probing for 16 s plus a 3 s recovery window. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case two-pair-stp-off-reboot-a-primary`.

| Phase | Expected | Limit | Run 1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pair A primary down | The surviving peer reverts its MLAG bonds to its own LACP system ID, so the other pair and the host renegotiate LACP, with brief loss. | ≤ 5 s | ❌ FAIL · 19.67 to 19.88 s · [failed check](01-two-pair-stp-off-reboot-a-primary/result.json#L383) |
| Pair A primary back | The rebooted switch keeps its bond members out of the bond until the MLAG system ID syncs (mlag-init-delay) and rejoins under the shared system ID. | ≤ 5 s | ✅ PASS · no loss |

- **Pair A primary down:** The surviving peer reverts its MLAG bonds to its own LACP system ID, so the other pair and the host renegotiate LACP, with brief loss. The virtual links keep carrier, so partners drop the member to the rebooted switch at the LACP short timeout (3 × 1 s). No port depends on spanning tree, no BPDUs appear, and each VLAN direction loses at most 5 s. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
- **Pair A primary back:** The rebooted switch keeps its bond members out of the bond until the MLAG system ID syncs (mlag-init-delay) and rejoins under the shared system ID. Partners renegotiate LACP with brief loss, and each VLAN direction loses at most 5 s. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
