### Pair A primary reboot · two MLAG pairs, spanning tree off, lacp-strict=yes on the inter-pair bond · STP off

Resets the data bridge on all four CHRs over management SSH with protocol-mode=none. It first sets lacp-strict=yes on the inter-pair bond of all four CHRs, so a member whose LACP partner times out stops carrying traffic. Pair A (CHR 1 MLAG primary, CHR 2 MLAG secondary) and pair B (CHR 3 MLAG primary, CHR 4 MLAG secondary) are joined by one distributed LACP bond, mlag-id 11 on pair A and 21 on pair B. Host 1 bonds to pair B and host 2 to pair A, so both VLAN 110 streams cross both pairs. It requires a loss-free baseline and zero BPDUs. It then reboots CHR 1 (pair A's MLAG primary) with `/system reboot`, waits for the guest uptime to reset and keeps probing for 16 s plus a 3 s recovery window. Run it with `mlag-lab run --case two-pair-stp-off-strict-reboot-a-primary`.

- **Pair A primary down:** The surviving peer reverts its MLAG bonds to its own LACP system ID, so the other pair and the host renegotiate LACP, with brief loss. The virtual links keep carrier, so partners drop the member to the rebooted switch at the LACP short timeout (3 × 1 s). No port depends on spanning tree, no BPDUs appear, and each VLAN direction loses at most 5 s. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)

Partners renegotiate LACP with brief loss, and each VLAN direction loses at most 5 s. [Source](https://manual.mikrotik.com/docs/high-availability-solutions/multi-chassis-link-aggregation-group/)
